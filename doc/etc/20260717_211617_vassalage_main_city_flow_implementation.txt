种火集结号 - 主城失守、附属、救出与主动脱离实现记录
Fireseed Engage - Capital loss, vassalage, rescue, and voluntary release implementation log

日期 / Date: 2026-07-17

一、规则落地 / Implemented rules

1. 敌对玩家彻底攻陷主城后，主城仍由原玩家经营，物理所有权不转移；
   系统会立即建立一条有效附属关系。
   A fully conquered capital remains physically owned and operated by its
   original player, while an active vassal relation is created immediately.

2. 关系同时记录直接宗主、当前势力领袖快照、原势力、原联盟身份、
   原贡献与加入时间，并保留 rescued、redeemed、replaced 历史。
   Relations record the direct lord, effective-force snapshot, former force,
   former alliance role, contribution, and join time, while retaining rescued,
   redeemed, and replaced history.

3. 附属玩家会暂时离开原联盟。首次失守时会冻结原势力可救援成员名单；
   名单内玩家之后无论离盟、入盟或成为其他附属，救出资格都不改变。名单
   内玩家再次攻陷主城时恢复原联盟与贡献；若原盟主职位已经移交，则以
   干部身份归队。
   A vassal temporarily leaves the former alliance. Eligible rescuers are
   frozen at the first capture, so later membership or vassalage changes do
   not rewrite eligibility. A listed player can reconquer the capital to
   restore membership and contribution; a transferred leader returns as an
   officer unless the leader seat is empty.

4. 非原势力玩家再次攻陷附属玩家主城时会改换直接宗主，但原势力快照
   继续保留，以便之后仍可由原势力救出。
   A later hostile conquest replaces the direct lord while retaining the
   original-force snapshot for a future rescue.

5. 附属期间，领地、城池和本季全部积分仍记在物理玩家账下，但排行榜、
   敌友判断、十二门通行权与赛季胜利会沿任意深度的直接宗主链实时汇总
   到当前有效势力。
   During vassalage, holdings and raw season scores remain on the physical
   account, while rankings, hostility, Gateway access, and season victory are
   attributed to the live effective force through an arbitrarily deep direct-
   lord chain.

二、主动脱离 / Voluntary release

1. 新页面 vassal.php 显示直接宗主、积分归属势力、原势力救出说明、
   六系贡金预估、迁城规则、领地数量和强制确认框。
   The new vassal.php page shows the direct lord, score force, rescue rules,
   six-resource tribute preview, relocation policy, holdings, and a mandatory
   confirmation.

2. 默认贡金为六系当前余额的 70%，每系向下取整并直接支付给直接宗主。
   计算使用百万分之一精度的定点数，避免 90 × 70% 被浮点误差算成 62。
   Tribute defaults to 70% of each live resource balance, floors each resource,
   and pays the direct lord. Millionth-scale fixed-point arithmetic prevents
   floating-point undercharges such as calculating 90 × 70% as 62.

3. 脱离事务会重新锁定并计算最新余额；宗主必须完整收到贡金，若整数上限
   无法容纳则整笔事务回滚，不会静默丢失资源。
   The release transaction recalculates locked live balances. The lord must
   receive the exact tribute; integer overflow rolls back the whole operation
   instead of silently dropping resources.

4. 迁城模式：
   - outer：地图最外围约 20% 带状区域；
   - middle：外围 20% 与中央 35%-65% 之间的中围环；
   - subbase：随机既有分基地升为主城；没有分基地时回退 outer。
   Relocation modes:
   - outer: the outermost approximately 20% map band;
   - middle: the ring between the outer 20% and central 35%-65% area;
   - subbase: promote a random existing sub-base, falling back to outer.

5. 开启全部领地清算时，普通领地、NPC 据点、特殊地点和未保留分基地
   会失去；城市士兵、领地驻军、驻城武将和军队会安全合并或召回新主城。
   With full settlement enabled, ordinary territory, NPC forts, special sites,
   and unkept sub-bases are released. City troops, territory garrisons, city
   generals, and armies are safely merged or recalled to the new capital.

6. 净领地分会按实际失去的普通领地、NPC 据点和计分分基地扣减。
   思考回路按仍占用成本的普通领地全额返还；这是归还既有投入，可暂时
   超过常规持有上限，之后的新产出会等待余额重新低于上限。分基地改建
   时已有的既有返还机制继续有效。
   Net territory score is reduced for lost ordinary tiles, NPC forts, and
   scored sub-bases. Circuit Points committed to ordinary territory are fully
   restored. Because this repays an existing investment, the balance may
   temporarily exceed its normal cap; new production waits until it falls
   below the cap. Ordinary rewards never reduce an over-cap refund, while
   abandonment and sub-base conversion also restore their investments in full.

7. 脱离不会恢复原联盟；玩家在迁城完成和贡金支付后成为独立玩家。
   救出才会恢复原势力身份。
   Voluntary release does not restore the former alliance. The player becomes
   independent after relocation and payment; only rescue restores the former
   force identity.

三、后台配置 / Administration settings

- vassal_release_resource_rate = 0.70
- vassal_release_relocation_mode = outer
- vassal_release_lose_all_territory = 1
- vassal_release_refund_circuit = 1

四、并发与完整性 / Concurrency and integrity

1. 用户、附属关系、联盟成员、军队、城池、地图和资源采用固定事务锁序。
   派兵、联盟创建/申请/审批与主城征服共享玩家优先锁，不能并发制造
   “既是附属又是联盟成员”或迁城后的陈旧战斗。战斗结算会预读、按ID
   锁定并再次复核参战双方的完整宗主链；链条在加锁前变化时整笔回滚重试。
   User, vassal, membership, army, city, map, and resource rows use stable lock
   ordering. Dispatch and alliance admission share user-first locks with
   capital conquest, preventing mixed vassal/membership states and stale
   relocation battles. Battle resolution previews, locks in ID order, and
   revalidates both complete lord chains; a pre-lock chain change rolls the
   transaction back for a clean retry.

2. 迁城会取消玩家发起、驻军防守、城池防守和领地防守的待处理战斗；
   外来进攻军会停止无效行军，玩家自己的军队统一召回。
   Relocation cancels pending battles involving the player's attacking army,
   defending army, city, or territory. Incoming armies stop invalid marches,
   and the player's own armies are recalled.

3. 有效附属唯一键由可空生成列实现。只有附属玩家删除可级联清理关系；
   宗主、势力领袖和原势力快照使用 RESTRICT，不能因演员删号静默解除
   有效附属或抹去历史。
   A nullable generated column enforces one active relation per vassal. Only
   deletion of the vassal cascades; lord, overlord, and former-force references
   use RESTRICT so actor deletion cannot silently free a vassal or erase history.

4. vassal_rescue_eligibility 以关系与玩家复合主键保存首次失守名单；
   再征服复制原名单。升级脚本会为旧有效关系按现存原联盟成员与已保存
   原势力领袖进行最佳努力回填。
   vassal_rescue_eligibility stores the first-capture roster by relation and
   user, and superseding conquests copy it. The upgrade best-effort backfills
   legacy active relations from current former-alliance members and the saved
   former-force owner.

5. 通用后台配置保存已修正为“更新保留原分类、新建默认 general”，
   因而附属配置保存后仍留在 vassalage 页签。
   Generic admin saves now preserve an existing category and use general only
   for new keys, so vassalage settings remain in their administration tab.
