种火集结号 - 内测准入与赛季机制实现规格（2026-07-19）
Fireseed Engage - Internal Beta Readiness and Seasonal Mechanics Specification (2026-07-19)

一、内测准入 / Internal beta gate

1. 修复会阻断排行、地图、军队、领地和后台登录流程的确定性故障。
   Fix deterministic failures blocking ranking, map, army, territory, and administrator login flows.
2. 修复后台存储型脚本注入风险，并执行维护模式、关闭注册和最大玩家数配置。
   Remove the stored-script-injection path in administration and enforce maintenance, registration, and player-cap settings.
3. 强化会话、登录节流、登出、CSRF、生产错误输出和服务器内部目录保护。
   Harden sessions, login throttling, logout, CSRF, production error output, and server-internal directory protection.
4. 地图生成必须在失败时完整回滚，不得提交或报告残缺世界。
   Map generation must roll back completely on failure and must never commit or report an incomplete world.

二、跨赛季资产 / Cross-season assets

1. 保留玩家账号、亮晶晶、夜静静、武将所有权与成长、技能卡及其成长、长期成长材料和永久科研。
   Preserve accounts, Bright and Night Crystals, general ownership and progression, skill cards and progression, long-term materials, and permanent research.
2. 保留同盟本体、基本资料、成员与职位。
   Preserve alliance identity, profile, membership, and roles.
3. 重置同盟等级、经验、成员贡献、申请、援助记录、协同作战、外交领地、附庸状态和赛季积分。
   Reset alliance level, experience, member contribution, applications, aid records, operations, territorial diplomacy, vassalage, and seasonal scores.
4. 技能点与长期成长道具保留；功勋与竞技场代币按赛季重置。
   Preserve skill points and long-term progression items; reset merit and arena tokens each season.

三、赛季资产与重建 / Seasonal assets and reconstruction

1. 重置暖洋洋、冷冰冰、郁萌萌、昼闪闪、思考回路余额、城市、设施、士兵、军队、领地、分基地、赛季科研及赛季玩法进度。
   Reset Warm, Cold, Green, and Day Crystals, current Circuit Points, cities, facilities, soldiers, armies, territories, sub-bases, seasonal research, and seasonal gameplay progress.
2. 每个新赛季重新生成资源点与 NPC 分布，并重新分配玩家主城。
   Regenerate resource and NPC distribution and reassign player main cities every season.
3. 十二门保持在地图十二个钟表方向，银白之孔保持在地图正中央。
   Keep the Twelve Gateways at the twelve clock directions and the Silver Hole at the exact map center.
4. 每次赛季重建必须原子完成；任何失败均保留旧世界和旧赛季状态。
   Every seasonal reconstruction must be atomic; any failure preserves the previous world and season state.

四、资源经济 / Resource economy

1. 亮晶晶和夜静静是可在游戏中获得、跨赛季保留的增值货币，不是付费货币。
   Bright and Night Crystals are earnable, cross-season value currencies and are not paid currencies.
2. 武将卡池只消耗亮晶晶；技能卡池只消耗夜静静；商店可同时使用二者。
   General pools consume only Bright Crystals; skill-card pools consume only Night Crystals; the shop may use both.
3. 普通建造、设施升级和赛季科研不得消耗亮晶晶或夜静静。
   Ordinary construction, facility upgrades, and seasonal research must not consume Bright or Night Crystals.
4. 主基地固定拥有亮、夜产出设施且可以升级；大地图亮、夜资源点显著少于其他四色资源点。
   Every main base includes upgradeable Bright and Night production facilities; Bright and Night world resource nodes are substantially rarer than the other four.
5. 每个新赛季向所有玩家发放一次可配置的亮、夜赛季开始奖励；新账号只领取一次账号初始资源。
   Grant every player one configurable Bright and Night season-start reward per season; new accounts receive the account-start grant only once.
6. 任务、成就和活动可以继续产出亮晶晶与夜静静。
   Quests, achievements, and events may continue to award Bright and Night Crystals.

五、科研与长期成长 / Research and long-term progression

1. 赛季科研使用四种赛季资源，效果必须实际接入生产、存储、训练、建造或战斗，并在赛季结束时重置。
   Seasonal research uses the four seasonal currencies, must affect production, storage, training, construction, or combat in runtime, and resets at season end.
2. 永久科研使用亮晶晶与夜静静，效果跨赛季保留。
   Permanent research uses Bright and Night Crystals and persists across seasons.
3. 玩家等级不再作为成长系统；数据库字段仅为兼容保留。
   Player level is no longer a progression system; its database field remains only for compatibility.
4. 思考回路上限、武将 COST 上限和分基地上限改为基础值加永久科研效果。
   Circuit capacity, general COST capacity, and sub-base capacity become base values plus permanent-research effects.

六、地图、侦察与占领 / Map, scouting, and occupation

1. 删除地图探索和战争迷雾；所有玩家开局即可查看全部地块的基础信息。
   Remove map exploration and fog of war; every player may view every tile's basic information from the start.
2. 敌方军队编成、驻军构成和城防细节继续由侦察系统保护。
   Enemy army composition, garrison composition, and detailed city defense remain protected by scouting.
3. 占领空地不消耗思考回路；占领资源地消耗可配置的思考回路。
   Occupying empty land costs no Circuit Points; occupying a resource tile costs a configurable amount.
4. 放弃资源地或将资源地改建为分基地时，全额返还其占领成本。
   Abandoning a resource tile or converting it into a sub-base fully refunds its occupation cost.

七、数值来源优先级 / Numeric source precedence

1. 原始角色卡与技能数据文件优先于企划文档，企划文档优先于当前 SQL 种子。
   Original general-card and skill data files take precedence over design documents, which take precedence over current SQL seeds.
2. 尚未完成专项讨论的数值必须集中配置，并使用可替换的临时基准，不得散落为不可追踪的魔法数字。
   Values awaiting dedicated balance review must be centrally configurable provisional baselines rather than scattered, untraceable magic numbers.
